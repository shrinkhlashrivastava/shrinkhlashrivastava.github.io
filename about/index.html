“Why do some interactions feel natural, while others feel frustratingly disconnected?”
What keeps me interested

Finding the structure underneath the interface.

That question has stayed with me through different types of products. Good design, to me, is not about making every surface louder or more novel. It is about understanding what people are trying to do, how the system behaves, and where the two stop matching.

That is why I tend to gravitate toward navigation problems, complex workflows, design systems and products with multiple roles or operational rules. There is usually something underneath the screen that needs to be made clearer first.

How I work with teams

Collaborative by default.

The work is rarely one designer and one perfect answer. The process gets better when product, design and development can challenge the same problem together.
