Hospitality · Responsive web

SAVOR Hospitality

Reframing a multi-venue hospitality website around discovery, reservation and a digital experience that felt closer to the brand’s physical spaces.

Timeline30-day sprint
Design ownershipIndependent website UX/UI design
CollaborationDeveloper handoff + implementation review
Post-launchWordPress content & design maintenance
The brief

Make a premium hospitality brand easier to explore and act on.

SAVOR manages multiple premium dining venues. The existing digital experience did not clearly communicate the master brand, help visitors move between venues or make high-intent actions — especially reservations, offers, loyalty and gifting — easy to find.

Design ownership covered the website audit, information architecture, responsive UX/UI and visual direction. Development was handled collaboratively, with continued support on the live WordPress site after launch.

01 · Audit & information architecture

Fix the journey before polishing the interface.

The audit focused on navigation, hierarchy, positioning and conversion. The new structure organised the site around the tasks visitors were most likely to arrive with: explore venues, book a table, discover events and offers, join loyalty or purchase a gift.

  • Clearer global navigation
  • Prominent Book a Table and Gift Card actions
  • Better relationship between master brand and individual venues
  • High-intent pathways surfaced earlier
02 · Venue discovery & reservation

Connect discovery to booking instead of treating them as separate tasks.

Venue pages were designed to bring together cuisine, location, ambience, occasion and booking context. Reservation entry points were kept visible and connected to venue information rather than sending visitors into dead ends.

  • Venue discovery by context and experience
  • Clear reservation CTAs
  • Google Maps / location integration
  • Venue detail → booking continuity
Show: Discover → Venue detail → Location → Reserve
03 · Retention & commercial journeys

The relationship should continue beyond a single reservation.

The redesigned experience gave more deliberate space to loyalty, first-party data capture, exclusive offers, events and digital gift vouchers while keeping the presentation aligned with a premium hospitality brand.

  • Membership / loyalty capture
  • Happy hour, AMEX, Asia Miles and seasonal offers
  • Digital gift vouchers across multiple values
  • Events and UGC / “Share Your Moments” concepts
Show: loyalty · offers · gift cards · UGC
04 · Visual direction

Luxury without becoming ornamental.

The visual direction used deep matte olive, muted champagne-gold accents, serif typography, deliberate whitespace and large hospitality imagery. The goal was editorial confidence rather than a generic restaurant-directory aesthetic.

  • Deep olive + restrained gold palette
  • Editorial typography and spacing
  • Photography-led storytelling
  • Responsive desktop and mobile system
05 · Live-site follow-through

Design did not stop at the mockups.

Developer collaboration continued through handoff and implementation, followed by selected WordPress maintenance. Working on the live site helped surface the difference between a polished design file and a system that needs to remain usable as content changes.

  • Developer handoff and design clarification
  • Responsive implementation review
  • WordPress content and visual maintenance
What this project demonstrates

Strategy, visual craft and conversion in one experience.

  • Independent ownership of the website design
  • Audit-led information architecture
  • Reservation, loyalty, offers and gifting connected into the experience
  • Premium responsive visual direction
  • Post-launch involvement on WordPress
