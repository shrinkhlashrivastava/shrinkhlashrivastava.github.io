The brief

Scale the product without scaling the confusion.

AngelPBX was growing beyond a single telephony product into a broader communication ecosystem. Existing interfaces had been built incrementally, and the experience needed stronger navigation, nomenclature, consistency and operational clarity as new modules and AI capabilities were added.

The engagement was collaborative across stakeholders, design and development. My contribution centered on product audits, information architecture, major parts of the core platform, design-system evolution, AngelMeet journey gaps and ongoing implementation QA.

01 · Product architecture

First, make the system easier to understand.

The platform side navigation and nomenclature were audited to identify confusing grouping, overlapping labels and gaps between related tasks. Recommendations reorganised modules around clearer operational categories and a structure that could grow with the ecosystem.

  • Navigation and nomenclature audit
  • Module grouping and hierarchy
  • Cross-product consistency
  • Scalable patterns for new functionality
02 · Shared design system

Create one product language across a growing ecosystem.

A shared component and interaction foundation helped prevent every new module from becoming its own design problem. The system covered recurring controls, data-heavy views, navigation patterns, panels, tables and common states used across the ecosystem.

  • Typography and colour foundations
  • Buttons, inputs and form patterns
  • Tables, panels and dense data states
  • Navigation and reusable interaction behaviour

04 · AI call intelligence

Move from call records to actual conversation intelligence.

Traditional call records answer when a call happened. The redesigned semantic CDR experience was shaped to help managers understand what happened — bringing recording, transcript, sentiment, scoring, key insights, training notes and summary into one review surface.

  • Natural-language semantic search
  • Transcript + recording in context
  • Sentiment, scoring and key insights
  • Training notes and summaries
  • AI bot for questions about calls
05 · AngelMeet

The meeting journey is bigger than the live call.

An audit of Creator and Participant journeys surfaced gaps before, during and after meetings. The design solutions connected scheduling and invites with the live meeting experience, then extended into recording, transcript, AI notes, summaries and action items.

  • Pre-meet: create · schedule · invite · pre-join
  • Live: collaboration · permissions · whiteboards · AI notes
  • Post-meet: recording · transcript · summary · actions
06 · AngelGo

Keep agent communication in one workspace.

AngelGo brought calls, WhatsApp, email, internal messaging, click-to-call leads and multiple assigned numbers into a single agent-facing experience. The redesign also considered mobile continuity, AI-assisted responses and supervisor intervention.

  • Desktop + mobile omnichannel workflows
  • AI suggested replies, summaries and next actions
  • Monitor · Whisper · Barge · Intercept
  • Mobile architecture designed with wider ecosystem support in mind
07 · From design to implementation

The design loop continued after handoff.

Developer questions, platform testing and repeated UX audits were part of the delivery process. Built screens were reviewed against intended behaviour, missing states and interaction details, then refined with the development team.

  • Developer clarification and handoff support
  • Functional testing and UX review
  • Design QA on implemented screens
  • Iterative fixes after build
