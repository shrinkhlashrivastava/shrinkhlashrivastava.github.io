A little about me

Curious about how things work.
Particular about how they feel.

Across recent roles, I’ve designed enterprise communication products, AI-agent platforms, mobile workflows, hospitality websites, IT service experiences and e-commerce journeys.

What keeps me interested is the same question that brought me into design in the first place: why do some interactions feel natural while others feel disconnected? I like finding the structure underneath that difference — and turning it into something useful, thoughtful and buildable.
