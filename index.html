What I bring

More than polished screens.

The strongest design work happens when structure, interaction, visual craft and implementation stay connected.

01

Start from the user’s mental model.

Organise products the way people think about their work, not the way the system was built.

02

Design for the moment of doubt.

Find where users hesitate or make mistakes, then make the next step clear and show problems before they cost anything.

03

Make it hold up in the real product.

Stay with the build through review and QA, so the experience users get matches the one that was designed.

A little about me

Curious about how things work.
Particular about how they feel.

Across recent roles, I’ve designed enterprise communication products, AI-agent platforms, mobile workflows, hospitality websites, IT service experiences and e-commerce journeys.

What keeps me interested is the same question that brought me into design in the first place: why do some interactions feel natural while others feel disconnected? I like finding the structure underneath that difference — and turning it into something useful, thoughtful and buildable.
